This Privacy Policy explains how AcrossMedia ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

Information we collect

Details you give us through our contact form, email or WhatsApp — such as your name, email, phone number, brand name, budget and project details.

Basic technical data collected automatically, such as browser type, device and pages visited, through privacy-friendly analytics.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare proposals and deliver our services.

To improve our website and understand which content is useful.

To send our newsletter, only if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing

We never sell your data. We share it only with trusted tools that help us run our business (for example, email and CRM providers), and only as needed.
