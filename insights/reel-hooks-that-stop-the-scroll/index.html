7 hooks that make people stop scrolling on Reels

AcrossMedia Creative Team28 Oct 20265 min read

Every reel is judged in its first second and a half. If the opening frame doesn't create a question, a feeling or a reason to stay, the thumb keeps moving. These are the seven hook patterns our creative team returns to again and again.

1. Start with the result

Show the finished dish, the transformed room or the sold-out sign first — then rewind to how you got there. People stay to see the journey.

2. Say the quiet part out loud

Open with an honest, slightly uncomfortable truth from your category: “Most skincare ads lie about results.” It earns attention and trust at the same time.

3. Pattern interrupts

A sudden zoom, an unexpected sound or an object entering frame breaks autopilot scrolling. Use it in the first 10 frames, not the tenth second.

4–7. Our quick-fire favourites

  • “Don't make this mistake if you…” (problem-aware audiences)
  • “POV:” framing that puts the viewer inside the story
  • A countdown or list that promises a payoff
  • Text-on-screen questions that can only be answered by watching
The hook isn't the first line of your script. It's the first frame of your video.

Test two or three hooks on the same body content. The winning hook often doubles watch time — with zero extra production cost.
